Mystic Marley Is “In Love” With Her Own Sound

Bob Marley's granddaughter blends reggae, R&B and a hip hop groove on her new single, with a voice that brings Erykah Badu to mind.

By Paolos Kidanemariam Music Journalist · November 28, 2026 · 3 min read

Mystic Marley's sound is reggae at the root, with R&B on top and a hip hop groove underneath. Her new single, “In Love,” released October 30, puts all three in one easy, swaying track. It feels modern without leaving Jamaica behind.

Then there is the voice. It is smooth, warm and a little smoky, and it reminds me of Erykah Badu: relaxed, soulful, never in a hurry. She doesn't push the song. She lets it come to her.

A Marley tune with her name in it

Before “In Love,” many fans first heard her on “Misty Morning,” her version of her grandfather Bob Marley's classic. It fits her perfectly. “Misty Morning” almost spells out her own name, Mystic, and she sings it like a song that was waiting for her. Her version appeared on Bob Marley: One Love – Music Inspired by the Film.

Did her father produce it?

We checked. Her father is Stephen Marley, the Grammy-winning artist and producer behind so much of the Marley family sound. He was executive producer of the One Love soundtrack album that carries her “Misty Morning.” But “In Love” is her own: it was produced by Yo Christon, and she wrote it with Ayaana Nash. You can hear the family in her phrasing, but this record is Mystic finding her own lane.

  • Song: “In Love”

The verdict: Smooth, soulful and her own. The next big Marley voice is already here.

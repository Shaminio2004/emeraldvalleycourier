FILES

  reggae.html            Reggae section
  world.html             World Music section
  bands.html             Bands section

  article-template.html  Copy this to write each new story
  styles.css             Colors, fonts and layout for every page
  images/                Your photos (paolos.jpg is your headshot)

PUT IT ONLINE (free)
  1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
  2. Drag this whole folder onto the page.
  3. You get a live link. To update later, drag the folder again
     (Site > Deploys > drag and drop).

ADD A NEW STORY
  1. Copy article-template.html and rename it,
     e.g. reggae-review-artist-name.html  (lowercase, dashes, no spaces)
  2. Open it in a text editor (Notepad / TextEdit) and replace the
     headline, summary, date, photo and text. Instructions are at the top.
  3. Put your photo in the images folder.
  4. Open the section page (e.g. reggae.html), find a card that says
     href="article-template.html" and change it to your new file name.
     Update that card's headline, summary, and change "Coming soon" to the date.
  5. Upload the folder to Netlify again.

Shaggy Opens Lottery With a Testimony: "God Is Amazing"

Introduced by the legendary dub poet Mutabaruka, the first track on Shaggy's new album blends reggae, hip hop and dancehall into a faith-driven anthem about rising above the doubters.

By Paolos Kidanemariam Music Journalist · October 22, 2026 · 3 min read
The only reason to look down on a man is to pick him up.

That idea runs through "God Is Amazing," the track that opens Shaggy's new album Lottery, released this year on VP Records.

It starts with a voice reggae fans will know right away: Mutabaruka, the legendary teacher, poet and dub-poetry pioneer. His introduction gives the song weight before the first beat drops. It sounds like an elder blessing the work.

A clever fusion

From there, Shaggy blends reggae, hip hop and dancehall into one fluid, faith-driven anthem. Australian singer Vanessa Amorosi adds a soaring pop edge. The message is resilience: let people talk, and keep shining anyway. The title phrase becomes a refrain you'll be repeating long after the track ends.

Bet on yourself

The album takes its name from a philosophy that has guided Shaggy's three-decade career: always bet on yourself. "God Is Amazing" sets that tone. Faith is the foundation, and confidence is the result.

The verdict: A clever, uplifting fusion and a strong opener. Don't skip this one on Lottery.

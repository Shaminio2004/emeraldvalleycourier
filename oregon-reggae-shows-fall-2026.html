Reggae Worth the Trip: Oregon Shows, October to December 2026

Festival season is over, but the reggae isn't. Here are the roots and island-sound shows worth the drive to Portland this fall, plus the dates to watch for 2027.

By Paolos Kidanemariam Music Journalist · October 17, 2026 · 4 min read

Oregon's big outdoor reggae festivals happen in summer, so between October and December the action moves indoors. The good news: some of the strongest names in reggae are coming through Portland this fall, and every one of these shows is an easy trip from Eugene up I-5.

Alborosie

Tuesday, October 20, 2026 · The Get Down at the Melody Event Center, Portland · 8 p.m.

The Sicilian-born, Jamaica-based singer and producer is one of the most respected roots voices of his generation, known for his deep studio craft and conscious message. A rare Pacific Northwest stop and the must-see show of the season.

Etana

Thursday, October 22, 2026 · Star Theater, Portland

"The Strong One" brings her powerhouse voice and roots-soul songwriting to the Star Theater. This is her only Pacific Northwest date on the current tour, so if you want to see her this year, this is the night.

Nahko and Medicine for the People

Wednesday, November 4 & Thursday, November 5, 2026 · Star Theater, Portland

Two nights of the HOKA 10th Anniversary Tour. Nahko's world-folk sound draws on reggae rhythms and a message of healing and unity, which has made him a favorite with the conscious reggae crowd across the West Coast.

Just missed it: Steel Pulse

Eugene got the biggest reggae night of the fall early: Steel Pulse brought the Reggae Against Racism World Tour 2026 to the McDonald Theatre on October 8. Read our backstage-and-onstage review and see the photo gallery.

Plan ahead for 2027

Kash'd Out is booked at the WOW Hall in Eugene on March 26, 2027. And keep an eye on Reggae Rise Up Oregon, the state's biggest reggae festival, which took place June 12 to 14 this year. The 2027 dates have not been announced yet; we'll post them as soon as they are.

At a Glance

  • Oct 20: Alborosie · The Get Down, Portland
  • Oct 22: Etana · Star Theater, Portland
  • Nov 4 & 5: Nahko and Medicine for the People · Star Theater, Portland
  • Mar 26, 2027: Kash'd Out · WOW Hall, Eugene
  • Summer 2027: Reggae Rise Up Oregon (dates TBA)

Dates and venues can change. Always confirm with the venue or official ticket seller before you buy, and avoid resale sites charging above face value.

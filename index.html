About Us

Paolos Kidanemariam

Music Journalist & Publisher
paoloskid@gmail.com

Emerald Valley Courier is an online music publication based in Eugene, Oregon, dedicated to reggae, roots and world music. We cover bands, artists, concerts and festivals with respect for the music and the cultures it comes from.

Founded by Paolos Kidanemariam, a reggae musician himself who has played and opened for touring acts, the Courier writes about music from the inside: the stage, the studio and the road.

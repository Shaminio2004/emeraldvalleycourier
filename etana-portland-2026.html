"The Strong One" Comes to Portland: Etana at the Star Theater

Two-time Grammy nominee Etana brings her roots-and-soul voice to Portland on Thursday, October 22, her only Pacific Northwest stop on this tour.

By Paolos Kidanemariam Music Journalist · October 17, 2026 · 3 min read

Few voices in modern reggae carry the warmth and power of Etana. Born Shauna McKenzie in Kingston, Jamaica, and raised in August Town, she moved to the United States as a child, sang in the group Gift, then returned to Jamaica to build a solo career rooted in Rastafari principles.

Her 2008 debut album, The Strong One, gave her the nickname that has followed her ever since, and songs like "Wrong Address" and "Roots" made her one of the leading women in reggae.

A catalog of strength

Etana has topped Billboard's reggae albums chart with I Rise (2014) and Reggae Forever (2018), the album that earned her a Grammy nomination for Best Reggae Album. Her more recent releases include Dimensions (2019), Pamoja (2021) and Nectar of the GOds (2024).

Why you should go

This is Etana's only Pacific Northwest date on the current run, so for Oregon fans, it's Portland or nothing this year. Expect a set that moves from roots anthems to soulful love songs, carried by one of the strongest voices in the music.

Show Details

  • Who: Etana
  • When: Thursday, October 22, 2026
  • Where: Star Theater, Portland, Oregon
  • Start here: "Wrong Address," "Roots"

Band in Review

Shengen Clan: this band will make your head nod

Alborosie's band brought Jamaican, uncut, super sound to The Get Down in Portland on October 20. Be careful: you may need a doctor.

By Paolos Kidanemariam Music Journalist · Emerald Valley Courier · October 21, 2026

This band will make your head nod. The drummer keeps time like a precise clock, a pendulum of meditation. The marching beat grooves through every roll, and the cymbal clashes land perfectly as the beat drops. Then comes the bass, like an earthquake, the sound of a marching army that snaps your neck to the left and snaps it to the right. Be careful: you may need a doctor. This is what reggae is. Jamaican, uncut, super sound.

Holding it all together is the keyboard player, Ottmar Campbell, painting the spaces between the drums and bass, while bassist and bandleader Michael "Megahbass" Fletcher and drummer Elisha "Drummerzeb" Ellis lock the rhythm section so tight it feels like one heartbeat. Behind Alborosie, Shengen Clan never just backs the singer. They lift him, push him and answer him, and by the end of the night the whole room was moving as one. It's the best band I've heard this year, and proof that real reggae is still played by real musicians, live, loud and full of spirit.

Shengen Clan

  • Band of: Alborosie
  • Bass & bandleader: Michael "Megahbass" Fletcher
  • Keyboards: Ottmar Campbell
  • Drums: Elisha "Drummerzeb" Ellis
  • Seen: The Get Down, Portland · October 20, 2026

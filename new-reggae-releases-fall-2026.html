New Reggae Releases: Shaggy, Lee "Scratch" Perry and Vybz Kartel

One out now, two on the way: a star-studded Shaggy album, unreleased Lee "Scratch" Perry recordings, and a year-end drop from Vybz Kartel.

By Paolos Kidanemariam Music Journalist · October 17, 2026 · 3 min read

Out now: Shaggy, Lottery

Shaggy's first album in six years is a 13-track, guest-packed affair released through VP Records and his own Ranch Entertainment. Sting returns for a reggae take on "Ain't No Sunshine," and Akon joins Aidonia on the lead single "Boom Body," which has passed 10 million streams. The guest list also includes Robin Thicke, Beres Hammond, Anthony Hamilton, Jeremih and Dexta Daps. Most of the album was produced by Shaggy with longtime collaborator Shane Hoosong.

Start with: "Boom Body" (feat. Akon & Aidonia), then the Sting duet.

Coming in November: Lee "Scratch" Perry, Scratch Is Alive

Five years after his passing, the dub pioneer's voice returns. Scratch Is Alive collects previously unreleased vocal and dub tracks recorded in Switzerland and London with English producer Steve Marshall, his fifth album with Perry. Expect the experimental Perry: reggae and dub mixed with soul, blues and electronic sounds. The CD is listed for November 20, 2026 on State of Emergency; some listings show an earlier digital date of November 13.

Coming December 18: Vybz Kartel, The Sun

Vybz Kartel's second album of 2026 is produced by Chambah and due Friday, December 18. Kartel has described it as a deluxe follow-up to this year's God & Time, and he has said Chris Brown will appear, along with other Afrobeats artists. No official tracklist has been released yet.

Release Calendar

  • Out now: Shaggy · Lottery (VP Records / Ranch Entertainment)
  • November: Lee "Scratch" Perry & Steve Marshall · Scratch Is Alive
  • December 18: Vybz Kartel · The Sun (prod. Chambah)

Nahko Comes Home: Two Nights of HOKA at the Star Theater

Portland-born Nahko Bear and Medicine for the People celebrate ten years of HOKA with two nights at the Star Theater, November 4 and 5.

By Paolos Kidanemariam Music Journalist · October 17, 2026 · 3 min read

For Nahko Bear, Portland is a homecoming. The frontman of Nahko and Medicine for the People was born here, and his band returns for two nights at the Star Theater on the HOKA 10th Anniversary Tour.

Formed in 2008, Medicine for the People blends world music, folk and reggae rhythms with lyrics about social and environmental consciousness. Nahko, of Native American, Puerto Rican and Filipino descent, has built much of his songwriting around identity, healing and reconnecting with his roots.

Ten years of HOKA

Released in 2016, HOKA sits at the center of a catalog that includes On the Verge (2012), Dark as Night (2013), Take Your Power Back (2020), beautiful trouble (2021) and TRENCHES (2023). The band has toured with Michael Franti, Xavier Rudd and SOJA, which is why they are a familiar name to West Coast reggae crowds.

Why you should go

An anniversary tour in the frontman's hometown, two nights in a row: if you are into the conscious, festival side of the reggae world, this is the run to catch.

Show Details

  • Who: Nahko and Medicine for the People
  • When: Wednesday, November 4 & Thursday, November 5, 2026
  • Where: Star Theater, Portland, Oregon
  • Tour: HOKA 10th Anniversary Tour

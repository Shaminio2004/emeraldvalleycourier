Alborosie Sets the Roof on Fire at The Get Down

Alborosie and Shengen Clan brought Nine Mile to Portland on October 20. Onstage and backstage with the king of organic, highly positive vibration.

By Paolos Kidanemariam Music Journalist · October 21, 2026 · 5 min read

Photo gallery: tap any picture to enlarge. Photos: Paolos Kidanemariam / Emerald Valley Courier.

The roof was on fire at The Get Down. Alborosie set it alight.

The music came first. Then his voice echoed through the room, and people gathered closer, as if to witness the arrival of a king. The bass trembled the floor, the drums announced his arrival, the keyboard and guitars proclaimed his coming. And then it happened: the king showed up, in kingly character.

Organic, magical, highly positive

What followed was organic and magical, a night of highly positive vibration. The crowd kept singing his songs through the night, every one of them, and as Alborosie kept vibrating, we all felt his magical energy penetrating our bones. I could not contain myself. I was dancing and singing to his catchy songs, full of that same positive vibration, right along with everyone else.

His band, Shengen Clan, deserves its own headline. Tight, powerful and locked in from the first note, they were the best band I have heard this year.

People gathered closer, as if to witness the arrival of a king.

Backstage, like a music video

After the show, backstage felt like a scene from a music video. Security ushered me in. The band sat together, and I saluted them like generals, with much respect. Then I walked further in, and there was Alborosie, sitting humbly with his bredrens.

I congratulated him on a very successful night, and we fist-bumped in respect. I took a few pictures, then asked one of his bredrens to take a photo of the two of us together. Backstage, he is exactly who he is onstage: organic, humble and highly positive.

"The music speaks for itself"

Earlier in the evening, I interviewed Alborosie. When I asked how a non-Jamaican earns acceptance as a reggae artist in Jamaica, his answer was simple: the music speaks for itself. That night in Portland, his music spoke to me loud and clear. The full interview is coming soon on the Emerald Valley Courier.

Quick Facts

  • Artist: Alborosie & Shengen Clan
  • Date: Tuesday, October 20, 2026
  • Venue: The Get Down, Portland, Oregon
  • Latest album: Nine Mile (Greensleeves / VP Records)

The verdict: organic, humble and highly positive, onstage and off. If Alborosie and Shengen Clan come anywhere near you, go, and be ready to dance.

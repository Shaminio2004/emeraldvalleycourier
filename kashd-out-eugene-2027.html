Kash'd Out Brings Florida Reggae Rock to the WOW Hall

The road-tested reggae rock band from Florida is booked for Eugene's WOW Hall on March 26, 2027. Here's who they are and why it's worth circling the date now.

By Paolos Kidanemariam Music Journalist · October 17, 2026 · 3 min read

Some bands build their audience online. Kash'd Out built theirs on the road. The Florida reggae rock band, fronted by singer Greg Shields with Jackson Hauserman on guitar, Joey Brohawn on bass, Marshall Hearne on drums and A-Mac on keys, has earned a reputation as one of the hardest-touring acts in the scene.

From Pepper's label to the Billboard charts

Kash'd Out signed with LAW Records, the label run by the band Pepper, and toured with Pepper and Less Than Jake before their debut album, The Hookup, even came out. Their music has reached the Billboard Reggae Charts, and their most recent album, Butter, was followed by the psychedelic-leaning single "Mushroom Tea" featuring Little Stranger, now on Ineffable Records. They have supported Collie Buddz, The Expendables and Iration, and headlined several national tours of their own.

Why you should go

The WOW Hall is one of Eugene's great rooms for this kind of band: close to the stage, loud and full of energy. A spring night with Kash'd Out is the perfect way to warm up for festival season.

Show Details

  • Who: Kash'd Out
  • When: Friday, March 26, 2027
  • Where: WOW Hall, Eugene, Oregon
  • Start here: "Mushroom Tea" (feat. Little Stranger)

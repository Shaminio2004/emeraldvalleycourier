Afrobeats & Dancehall Now: Rema, Popcaan, Perfect Giddimani and More

A new Rema EP, Popcaan's message of resilience, Perfect Giddimani's run of 2026 singles, and the Afrobeats records that ruled the year.

By Paolos Kidanemariam Music Journalist · Emerald Valley Courier · November 26, 2026

From Lagos to Kingston, the sound of the moment moves between two capitals. Here is what is new, and what is worth your time, on both sides of the water.

Rema: E-Mortal Boy

Rema is back with E-Mortal Boy (EMB), a seven-track EP released Friday, November 20. It is his first EP since Ravage (2023) and follows his 2024 album HEIS. Lead singles "Oh No," produced by Rvssian and London, and "TEA" set the tone: sharp, melodic and built for the dance floor.

Popcaan: "Life Still A Gwaan"

The Unruly boss teamed with producer Damage Musiq for "Life Still A Gwaan," a song about holding on through hard times. Released in April, it debuted on Billboard, passed five million YouTube views and reached No. 3 on YouTube in Jamaica. It comes after his fifth album, Nothing Without God (November 2025), and a feature on Drake's 2026 album Maid of Honour. This month Vybz Kartel put feud talk to rest: "There is no rival."

Perfect Giddimani: roots and culture, single by single

Perfect Giddimani is also one of our five reggae artists to hear this month. He has spent 2026 releasing music on his own Giddimani Records: "Red & Dread" with Sinky Beatz in February, "Chaaba & Dudukwe" in March, and "Reggae 101," again with Sinky Beatz. The Jamaican singjay keeps his message conscious and his delivery fierce, a reminder that dancehall energy and roots values still live in the same voice.

Afrobeats records of the year

Wizkid & Asake, "Turbulence": two of Nigeria's biggest names on one track, which passed 20 million Spotify streams in its first month.

MOLIY & Tyla, "Body Go": Ghana meets South Africa. The song debuted at No. 2 on the UK Afrobeats chart, a record debut for a Ghanaian artist.

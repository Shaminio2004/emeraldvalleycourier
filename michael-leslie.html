Michael Leslie: a Eugene artist you should know

Inside the Lightcode Records studio with the singer and bandleader behind Michael Leslie & the Lightcode Band. Full interview and his new single, coming soon.

By Paolos Kidanemariam Music Journalist · Emerald Valley Courier · October 21, 2026

Entering his palace was like entering a small church in Ethiopia: a place where His Majesty Haile Selassie would be pleased to sit and meditate. Selassie was in the room that day.

Michael Leslie welcomed me with open arms and a smile only a longtime friend would give. I began to observe his rhythm section, laid out as if a concert was about to happen: a vocal space, keyboards and controls. It was a spaceship control room.

He sat. Snap, I took a picture. He began to reason. His Irish roots lead back to Africa, he murmured. His connection with Ethiopia and His Majesty is undeniable. He has played his part in bringing reggae to Eugene since he was 17.

Then we sat down to the interview.

Who is Michael Leslie?

Michael Leslie is a Eugene, Oregon-based reggae singer and bandleader who fronts Michael Leslie & the Lightcode Band. Known for soulful, conscious roots reggae, he has become a fixture of the Oregon reggae scene, playing the Oregon Reggae Fest in Ashland and headlining Southern Oregon's Spring Awakening festival. In 2025 he opened for reggae legend Barrington Levy at Portland's Aladdin Theater.

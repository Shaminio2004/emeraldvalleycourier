Etana Gives Bob Marley's "I Shot the Sheriff" a Soulful New Life

Over Bob Marley's rhythm arrangements, "The Strong One" proves she is the soul of reggae: a clear, pleasant voice that leaves you wanting more.

By Paolos Kidanemariam Music Journalist · October 22, 2026 · 2 min read

Soulful. That is the first word that comes to mind when Etana sings over Bob Marley's rhythm arrangements on her cover of "I Shot the Sheriff."

Marley wrote and recorded the song with the Wailers in 1973, and he called it a metaphor for justice. Etana keeps that message and the roots of the original, then strips the song back to its emotional core. Her vocal warmth sits right on top of the riddim, and she makes the song her own.

The soul of reggae

Etana is the soul of reggae. Her voice is clear and pleasant, never forced, and every line lands. This is the kind of singing that makes you want to hear more and more from this strong woman.

She released the single on her own independent label, Freemind Music Records, as a tribute to Marley to close out Reggae Month 2026 in Jamaica, where she served as the first Reggae Month Ambassador.

Here to stay

Etana will definitely be around for a long time. Two Grammy nominations, chart-topping albums and now a Marley cover that honors the original without copying it: "The Strong One" keeps earning her name.

Quick Facts

  • Song: "I Shot the Sheriff" (Bob Marley cover)
  • Artist: Etana
  • Label: Freemind Music Records
  • Released: February 2026, Reggae Month
  • Original: Bob Marley & the Wailers, 1973
  • See her live: Tonight, Thursday, October 22, Star Theater, Portland. Show details →

The verdict: Soulful, respectful and beautifully sung. Put it on repeat.

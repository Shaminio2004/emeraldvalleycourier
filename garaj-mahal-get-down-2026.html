Garaj Mahal Brings Jazz, Funk and Indian Fusion to The Get Down

Guitar wizard Fareed Haque and bassist Kai Eckhardt lead the world-fusion band into Portland on Thursday, November 19, touring their first album in 15 years.

By Paolos Kidanemariam Music Journalist · October 30, 2026 · 3 min read

Few bands blend the world's music as fearlessly as Garaj Mahal. Since 2000, the group has mixed jazz, rock, funk and Indian music into something all its own, built on long, adventurous jams and serious musicianship.

Two masters at the core

At the center are guitarist Fareed Haque, known for bringing the sound of the sitar to the electric guitar, and bassist Kai Eckhardt. Their 2005 album Blueberry Cave won Best Jam Album at the Independent Music Awards, and songs like "Bicycling in Bombay" and "Celtic Indian" show just how far their musical map stretches.

Back with Rotifer

After stepping away from touring in 2010, the band returned to the stage in 2018. In 2025 they released Rotifer, their first album in 15 years, with guests Angelo Moore of Fishbone and Cautious Clay. Funk City called the band "one of the most sophisticated funk-jazz-world-fusion bands of the past 25 years."

Why you should go

If you love world music that takes risks, this is a rare chance to hear Garaj Mahal up close in one of Portland's best rooms. Expect new songs from Rotifer alongside the classics.
